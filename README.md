# RoundModel: Little Prince

A 3D model of Prince, a small, very round, white, fluffy rabbit, built to be
the body of the "Little Self" project.

How it's built: a parametric model of Prince, fitted numerically to his photos,
with each part (head, undercarriage, ears, peets, fluff, wisps, whiskers) shaped
in its own step and reviewed by his owner. The front photo
(`data/photos/anchor_front.webp`) is the ground truth.

- `CLAUDE.md`: rules for every session. Start here.
- `docs/DECISIONS.md`: every decision and the reasons behind it.
- `docs/STRAND_PLAN.md`: the current step.
- `docs/PLAN.md`: the overall pipeline. `docs/PRINCE_SPEC.md`: what he looks like.
- `docs/PROGRESS.md`: the running log.
