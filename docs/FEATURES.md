# What makes him Prince: features to preserve (from the owner's intents)

Gathered from docs/INTENTS.md, the owner's messages and his photos. Every
build is checked against this list, master first, then the side, then the
others. Each line says what he has and, where it went wrong before, what
he does NOT have. The master photo is the immutable reference for how he
looks from the front.

## Whole rabbit

1. Very round, compact, soft: a white Dwarf Hotot loaf; fluffy fur, so his
   outline is soft, never hard-edged.
2. Not crooked: in the master he looks straight forward at the camera, his
   midline vertical through the middle of his face (maybe a touch more
   weight on his right peet).
3. One smooth surface: no blobs, ripples, ridges, creases, flaps, seams,
   walls or shards; proper curves.
4. Real proportions from every side, not only from the front: what matches
   the master from the front must also look like him from the side, above
   and behind.

## Head

5. Round, fuller and higher above the eyes; wider above and beside the eyes
   up to the ears; full cheeks.
6. Forehead runs in one soft line into his nose (no step, no bridge).
7. From the side his face slopes back from the nose, and his head is round
   behind it (not a flat, upright face plate).
8. His head is distinct from his body: a neck you can make out (chin, a soft
   dip, then the bib), not one shape running from mouth to chest.

## Eyes

9. On the sides of his head, bulging outward; seen past the curve of his
   cheeks from the front (NOT in frontal sockets under brow ridges).
10. Profile shows the eye's full circle; from the front and three-quarters it
    is foreshortened and his brow, forehead and fur hide part of it.
11. Set closer together than the old model had them (every front photo agreed).
12. Dark, with the Hotot black ring/lashes round them.

## Nose, muzzle, mouth, expression

13. A small nose (NOT a narrow, ridged, pointed human nose).
14. A broad, soft, gently sloping muzzle, best seen from the side.
15. A short, soft split under the nose; his mouth is a rabbit's, NOT a
    closed human upper lip.
16. Whisker pads: two rounded, puffy cushions around the split (NOT carved
    lip lobes curving down like smile lines).
17. Expression: neutral, curious, a little mysterious, uniquely rabbit (NOT
    a peaceful smile, NOT a "Victorian death mask").

## Chin, chest, peets

18. Chin, soft dip, then his bib coming forward smoothly; no crease under
    the chin.
19. Front peets fluffy and soft, tucked under him in a loaf; in the master
    (standing) they show at the bottom, rounded (NOT small separate lumps
    stuck on).

## Ears

20. Small, short, rounded, upright, set wide on top of his head; bigger than
    the old model's; facing forward-outward.

## From above and behind

21. From above: an oval (his circumference), nose at the front, not squared.
22. From behind: round, his back rounded, haunches round, a tail.

## How he is judged

- By eye, against his photos, master first; numbers only as a minor check.
- Every photo is an ordinary photo of a moving rabbit: pose, posture,
  head turn, light and fur differ between them, and that is normal.
- If a result looks wrong or reads as "not a rabbit" from any side, stop
  and say so.
